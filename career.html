Discover The Career

Join Bugatti and become part of a world where innovation, craftsmanship and performance come together. Bugatti Rimac combines the heritage of Bugatti with modern automotive technology and engineering. Working with Bugatti means contributing to extraordinary hyper sports cars while being part of a team focused on excellence, creativity and innovation.

1. Joining Bugatti

Becoming part of Bugatti means more than working for a luxury automotive brand. It means joining a team committed to engineering, craftsmanship and automotive excellence. Bugatti's headquarters in Molsheim, Alsace provides an inspiring working environment where employees contribute to the development of some of the world's most extraordinary hyper sports cars.

2. Innovation and Growth

Bugatti encourages employees to think boldly, experiment with new technologies and solve complex engineering challenges. Team members have opportunities to develop their professional skills while contributing to projects involving performance, technology and automotive design. Career opportunities may include professional roles, internships and work-study programs.

3. Values and Work Environment

Excellence, passion and dedication are important elements of Bugatti's working culture. Employees work together in a collaborative environment where creativity, precision and continuous learning are encouraged. Every project provides an opportunity to contribute to the future of hyper sports cars and deliver extraordinary automotive experiences.

4. Unique Location and Opportunities

Bugatti's historic headquarters are located in Molsheim, Alsace. The location combines automotive heritage, engineering excellence and a unique working environment. Career opportunities can include roles in engineering, manufacturing, information technology, sales, business development and other areas. Internships and professional development opportunities can also help young professionals build valuable experience.
